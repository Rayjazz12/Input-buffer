# Input Buffer

Normally Geometry Dash only counts a click if it lands on an exact frame.
**Input Buffer** saves a click you made a little too early and fires it on the
**first frame it becomes valid**, so tight orbs and landings stop eating your
inputs.

## How to use
1. Press the blue **IB** button in the main menu or pause menu, or press
   **Shift** in either menu.
2. Pick a buffer window: **Off**, **50 / 90 / 130 / 180 / 250 ms**.
3. Choose what it applies to: **Orbs**, **Ground** (landings), **Practice**
   (only works in practice mode), **Flash** (a dot when a saved click fires)
   and **HUD** (your waiting clicks in the corner).
4. Pick a theme: Ocean, Sunset, Mint, Grape, Rose or Midnight.

## Notes
- Up to 3 early clicks can wait at once, one click per orb or landing.
- Clicks older than your window are thrown away, so it never fires late.
- One landing uses one click: standing on the ground will not drain the queue.
- The buffer pauses while you hold the button, so it never fights your input.
- This is a helper mod. It will not be accepted for official records, so use
  **Practice** mode if you want to stay legit.
