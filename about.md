# Input Buffer

Normally Geometry Dash only counts a click if it lands exactly when an action is allowed. **Input Buffer** saves a click you made a little too early and fires it on the **first frame it becomes valid**, so tight orbs and landings stop eating your inputs.

## How to use
1. Press the blue **IB** button in the main menu or the pause menu, or just press **Shift** in either menu.
2. Pick a buffer window: **Off**, **50 / 90 / 130 / 180 ms**, or **250 ms (Max)**.
3. Choose where it applies: **Orbs**, **Ground** (landings), **Practice** (only works in practice mode) and **Flash** (a small dot when a saved click fires).
4. Pick a **theme**: Ocean, Sunset, Mint, Grape, Rose or Midnight.

## Notes
- Up to 3 early clicks can wait at once, so spamming into back-to-back orbs works. One click is used per orb or landing.
- Early clicks older than your chosen window are thrown away, so it never fires a random click.
- This is a helper mod. It will not be accepted for official records, so use **Practice** mode if you want to stay legit.
